# Insurance And Storage Guidance

General guidance only:

- Photograph both sides of each coin.
- Record acquisition date, source, purchase price, and storage location when known.
- Store coins away from humidity and direct handling.
- Use holders meant for coin preservation.
- For high-value items, consider professional grading or appraisal.
- For coverage questions, contact an insurance provider.

CoinBuddy should help prepare records, not decide coverage.
