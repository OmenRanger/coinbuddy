# CoinBuddy Product Brief

## Product

CoinBuddy is a standalone, local-first coin collection app for simple inventory, photo records, estimated value ranges, education, and archive exports.

## Primary User

The first user is the collector's mother: capable, curious, and not interested in technical setup. The app should feel obvious from the home screen, with large choices and friendly language.

## Promise

CoinBuddy helps a collector answer five everyday questions:

- What coins do I have?
- Where are they stored?
- What might they be worth?
- Which records would help with insurance or estate planning?
- What should I learn before handling, storing, grading, or selling?

## MVP Scope

The MVP supports manual coin entry, scanner-style image upload, collection browsing, coin detail records, editable value ranges, comparable sale notes, archive exports, education, and curated auction/source links.

## Not In MVP

- Automated coin identification
- Official grading
- Official appraisal
- Insurance coverage decisions
- Scraping third-party auction sites
- Cloud sync

## Product Principles

- Make the first action obvious.
- Use careful wording around value, grade, and insurance.
- Keep every record exportable.
- Favor human-readable fields over database language.
- Confirm destructive actions.
- Keep the interface warm, calm, and uncluttered.
